What is the Class Broadcast System?

In one line: it turns the computer in your classroom into a notice board that speaks.

It is a web tool for schools. From any laptop or phone with internet access, open the teacher console in a browser, type a sentence and hit send. The computer in the classroom instantly shows the notice full-screen and reads it out loud.

Students need no phones or tablets, and there is nothing to install. Open the classroom page on the class computer and enter the 8-character bind code — that is the pairing done. After that, teachers can reach the whole class without leaving the office.

  • Teacher console (web page) Opens in any browser on a phone or office computer — send notices, check the history.
  • Classroom page (class computer) One page kept open on the classroom computer; it shows the pop-up and speaks the notice.
  • Server The relay between the two: accounts, classes, bind codes and notice delivery.

Frequently asked questions

Does the classroom computer have to stay on?

Yes. Keep the classroom page open on that computer (an all-in-one panel or an older machine works well) so notices appear instantly.

Why is there no sound when a notice appears?

Browsers only allow audio after the user interacts with the page. Tap or click once on the classroom page after binding, and voice will work from then on.

Can I send notices from my phone?

Yes. The teacher console is a web page — sign in from a mobile browser and you can send notices and check the history.

How many teachers can send notices to one class?

As many as you like. The class owner sends an invite code and other teachers register or join with a co-teacher code.
